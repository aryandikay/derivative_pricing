HYBRID DERIVATIVE PRICING EVALUATION REPORT
===========================================

SECTION 1: SYSTEM OVERVIEW
--------------------------
The hybrid pricing architecture combines a neural network (NN) surrogate, an uncertainty estimator, and a router that selects between the NN and exact Black-Scholes pricing.

- NN surrogate purpose: approximate the Black-Scholes call price rapidly across the moneyness/time/volatility/rate surface.
- Uncertainty estimation role: quantify model confidence at each input and support safe fallback decisions.
- Router decision logic: route to the NN when predicted uncertainty is below a threshold (tau) and fall back to exact Black-Scholes otherwise.

Goal: achieve a practical speed-accuracy tradeoff by using the NN for most executions while preserving pricing accuracy through uncertainty-gated exact fallback.

SECTION 2: DATA DESCRIPTION
---------------------------
The evaluation uses the processed test dataset contained in `data/processed/test.npz`.

- Samples: 15,000 test points.
- Features: [S/K (normalized spot-to-strike ratio), T (time to maturity), sigma (implied volatility), r (risk-free rate)].
- Preprocessing: inputs are transformed by a saved scaler (`models/input_scaler.pkl`) before being passed to the NN surrogate. The exact Black-Scholes comparator operates on raw price inputs.

SECTION 3: CORE ACCURACY METRICS
--------------------------------
Metrics computed on the full test set.

- MAE (Mean Absolute Error): 0.000858. This is the average absolute pricing error normalized by strike.
- RMSE (Root Mean Square Error): 0.001154. RMSE penalizes larger deviations more strongly and is useful for capturing occasional outliers.
- MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error): 3.4659% after excluding near-zero true prices (true price > 1e-3). This measures relative pricing accuracy in economically relevant regions.

Interpretation:
- MAE at the sub-basis-point level is strong for a normalized pricing surface.
- RMSE remaining close to MAE indicates the error distribution lacks extreme catastrophic outliers across the test set.
- MAPE below 3.5% in the filtered region is acceptable for many surrogate pricing applications, though risk-sensitive use cases may demand tighter control.

SECTION 11: CRITICAL DIAGNOSIS
------------------------------
What is working well:
- The NN surrogate delivers strong global accuracy and preserves distributional moments.
- The ATM and overall pricing metrics are consistent with a viable Black-Scholes approximation.
- The uncertainty regressor has a meaningful signal, with higher uncertainty associated with larger realized error.

What is failing or fragile:
- The router is over-reliant on the NN path: exact fallback is not engaged.
- The threshold appears miscalibrated or too low for the current uncertainty scale.
- Tail region analysis shows a slight upward bias and elevated risk, making robust fallback desirable.

Key issue:
- The system is effectively operating as a pure surrogate rather than a hybrid uncertainty-gated system.

SECTION 12: RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------
Actionable improvements:
- Recalibrate the uncertainty threshold (`tau`) so that some exact fallback is triggered in high-risk regions.
- Improve uncertainty calibration using additional loss terms, adversarial examples, or ensemble-based uncertainty if needed.
- Implement risk-aware routing that prioritizes exact pricing for low-price / high-volatility points rather than relying solely on a single threshold.
- Add targeted tail robustness training or loss weighting to reduce overestimation in the <0.01 price band.
- Track fallback coverage explicitly during validation and tune for a desired operating point (e.g. 80% NN, 20% exact) rather than 100% NN.

SECTION 13: FINAL CONCLUSION
----------------------------
The surrogate model quality is strong: global MAE, RMSE, and MAPE are consistent with a high-fidelity NN approximation of the Black-Scholes surface.

However, the hybrid system is not yet production-ready in its current configuration because the router fails to exercise the exact fallback branch. The uncertainty model is promising, but the decision boundary must be recalibrated to realize a genuine speed-accuracy tradeoff.

Recommendation: retain the NN surrogate as the core engine, then re-tune the router and uncertainty criterion before declaring the architecture production-viable.
